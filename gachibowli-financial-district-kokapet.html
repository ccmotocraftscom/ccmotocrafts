Questions customers ask

Quick answers before you book.

Is pickup and drop really free?

Yes, in our listed areas. Enter your pincode at the top of the page to check. Outside these areas, message us on WhatsApp for the pickup charge.

Will you change any part without asking me?

No. We send an itemised parts and labour quotation on WhatsApp. No part is replaced until you approve it.

What guarantee do I get?

30 days on general service and 1 year on complete engine work.

Which bikes do you service?

All brands from 100cc to 2500cc, geared and gearless, including scooters and premium bikes.

How fast do you collect my bike?

For emergency pickups our team is dispatched in 35 to 45 minutes. For scheduled pickups we arrive in the slot you choose.

How do I see my service history?

Use Track my bike with your booking code and mobile number to see dates, kilometres, work done and charges.
